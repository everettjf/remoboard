From desk to phone in three steps.

No account and no companion desktop install. Your iPhone hosts a tiny local web page; the computer you already use becomes the keyboard.

01

Enable the keyboard

Add Remoboard in iOS Settings and allow Full Access so the keyboard can accept a local connection.

02

Open the address

Switch to Remoboard on your iPhone, then open the local address it shows in any modern desktop browser.

03

Pair and type

If PIN pairing is enabled, enter the six-digit code shown on your phone. Your text then travels directly across your local network to the active field.
